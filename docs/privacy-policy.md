## What the extension stores

The extension stores two pieces of information, both created by your use of the
extension itself:

- **A click count** — the number of times you have pressed the count button in the
  extension's popup. It is kept in the browser's local extension storage on your device.
- **A display setting** — whether the toolbar icon shows that count. It is kept in the
  browser's synced extension storage.

The extension does not read the content of the web pages you visit. On pages at
example.com it adds a small label saying it is running; it reads nothing from those
pages and records nothing about your visit.

## Where it goes

- The click count never leaves your device.
- The display setting is synced by your browser to the other browsers where you are
  signed in with the same account, if you have browser sync turned on. That sync is
  performed by your browser vendor (Google for Chrome, Microsoft for Edge) under their
  own privacy terms. With sync turned off, the setting stays on your device.
- Nothing is sent to the developer or to any other party. The extension contains no
  analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no remote code, and it makes no network
  requests of its own.

## What it does not do

- It does not sell or transfer your information to anyone.
- It does not use your information for any purpose other than counting clicks and
  showing the count.
- It does not use your information to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

## Keeping and deleting your information

Both values stay until you remove them. Uninstalling the extension deletes the click
count from that device. The display setting may remain in your browser account's sync
data; clearing your browser's synced data removes it.
